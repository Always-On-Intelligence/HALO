## Eliminate Factually Incorrect Options

1. For each option, identify any claims about video content (actions, objects, relationships).
2. Verify each claim against what you observe in the video:
   - Is this action actually shown? (e.g., "consuming flowers" vs. "stringing flowers")
   - Is this object actually present? (e.g., "vase" vs. "bag")
   - Is this relationship accurate? (e.g., "conversation" vs. "playing cards")
3. Eliminate any option with even one factually incorrect element.
4. Among remaining options, select the most precise match.

**Example:** Option B says "puts them in a vase" but video shows flowers being strung. Eliminate B. Option E says "puts them on a string" — this is factually correct, so select E.

**Anti-pattern:** Selecting an option that sounds reasonable or well-written without verifying its factual accuracy against the actual video content. Always prioritize correctness over plausibility.
